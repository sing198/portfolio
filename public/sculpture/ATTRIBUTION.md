# Kinetic sculpture asset

**Infinite, 3D Head Scan** by **Lee Perry-Smith**.

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The original license notice is retained in `LeePerrySmith_License.txt`.

Source: [official Three.js example assets](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith).
Original work: [Triplegangers](https://www.triplegangers.com/), with additional permissions through [Infinite Realities](https://www.ir-ltd.net/).

Included assets:

- `LeePerrySmith.glb` — original head scan geometry, downloaded from the official Three.js repository.
- `head-normal.jpg` — original `Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg`, renamed locally.

Portfolio adaptation: the scan is finished in silver and graphite, lit with neutral studio lights and cool edge highlights, and animated into separated facets around a blue crystalline core. Its original photo color map is not used.
